EDF-X5 Sport Jet

A 3D-printed sport jet built around one 50 mm ducted fan on 6S. A chin inlet feeds an S-duct to the fan in the tail, and the swept wing splits into printable panels. Every part was modeled in Fusion 360 by Claude from a written brief.

TailplaneVh 0.44Root chord 148 mm, tail arm 387 mm
FinVv 0.050Root chord 163 mm, tail arm 394 mm

Controls are ailerons, elevators and a rudder on 15 nylon hinges, driven by 4 servos. No flaps: a cambered airfoil gets most of the low-speed lift without a second pair of moving surfaces.

03

Propulsion & airflow

InletChinLip at 192 mm from the nose, 102% of the fan's swept area
DuctS-ductRises from the chin to the fan axis at only 3.7°, so the flow stays attached
Nozzle91%FSAExit at 1046 mm, printed as its own part
Installed thrust≈931gfBench figure corrected for pack voltage sag under 38 A and duct losses

Why a chin inlet: side inlets had no room for a gentle S-bend, and a nose inlet would have taken the space the battery needs to balance the aircraft. In the 3D view, the lines through the fuselage show air entering the chin inlet, turning up through the duct, swirling through the fan and leaving as a fast jet. Turn on X-ray to see them inside.

04

Mass

CAD audit
All-up weight, measured from the model≈1380 g
  • Printed parts817 g
  • Battery, 6S 1300215 g
  • Fan, ESC, servos, radio177 g
  • Spars, hinges, linkages, joints83 g
  • Landing gear60 g
  • Wiring and connectors28 g

Over target by 40%. The design aimed at 984 g. Weighing every printed part from its real volume in the model gives ≈1380 g: the wing alone is 353 g and the fuselage shell 301 g. Getting the weight down is the first job before printing.

These figures use the model-measured weight, not the target. At the 984 g target the thrust-to-weight is about 0.95. The airflow in the 3D view is a simple model drawn around the CAD surfaces. It shows the flow pattern; it is not wind-tunnel or CFD data. Not flight-tested.

06

Printing

26 parts
Fuselage6 segmentsPrinted standing up. The cuts avoid the spar box, the wing bolts and the fan socket, and the fan comes out with one segment.
Wing4 panels + 2 tipsPrinted solid, spanwise, with 6% grid infill that stacks into vertical stringers, on a 10 mm carbon spar
Ring frames5 × 2mmAt every fuselage cut they tie the duct to the skin and give a glue flange. Without them the duct would float loose inside the segments.
Printer256mm bedEvery part passes an automatic bed-fit check in the build, some only on the 45° diagonal

Plain PLA, 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.55 mm line width to match the skin, 2 walls, no supports.
