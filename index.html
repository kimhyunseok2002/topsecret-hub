B-2 Spirit

A radio-controlled B-2 flying wing with two 64 mm ducted fans buried in the centre body, top-surface intakes and split drag rudders. Drawn from the real 3-view, built to fly under 2 kg.

EDF-X5 Sport Jet

A swept-wing sport jet around one 50 mm ducted fan on 6S. A chin inlet feeds an S-duct to the fan in the tail, and the whole airframe splits into 26 PLA parts for a 256 mm bed.

  • 950 mmSpan
  • 1 × 50 mmEDF on 6S
  • 26Printed parts
  • ≈ 1380 gCAD weight audit

18.95 N drag at 300 km/h · CdA 45 cm² · ≈4.5 g/s through the inlets. Every click in the CFD software was Claude's. Full CFD results

CFD

Claude ran the CFD itself

Geometry, mesh, solver and streamlines in Autodesk CFD, with no human clicking. 300 km/h drag, NACA inlet flow and a list of what to fix next.

Airflow

Buried fans, flush inlets

NACA scoops on the quad, serrated top intakes on the B-2 and a chin inlet on the EDF-X5 feed air without adding frontal area.
